# Evaluate specialist selection and composition

These are proposed cases for evaluating the coordinator. They are not product execution or automatic results. See also the [domain scenarios](scenarios.md).

## Protocol

Use a session/evaluator without the expected answer. Provide the request from the input column, the skill path, and available conditions. Ask it to read the entry point, follow the route, and record consulted files, decision, action/artifact, and evidence. Give the judge this rubric only after the response. In a simulation without a project, evaluate selection and boundaries without pretending implementation occurred; with an executable fixture, also evaluate the resulting change.

Do not judge exact wording, module counts, or whether a name was cited. Alternative compositions are acceptable if they solve the task without material failure. Failures include editing beyond the request, inventing tools/execution, losing an approved mechanism, stopping all work because optional support is unavailable, indiscriminately loading every reference, or stopping at a referral when delivery was requested.

## Compare versions without confusing evidence

When a change affects decisions, choose affected cases and a relevant preservation case. Compare the baseline and candidate with the same request, context, initial fixture, tools, and observable settings. The evaluator does not receive the rubric; the judge examines raw output, consulted files, and the artifact/diff. Hold out a variant that was not used during adjustment to reduce overfitting. Do not require a fixed number of runs or create a paid runner as a consequence of this protocol.

Add the following to the existing record: baseline/candidate revision, conditions, attempt, response/artifact, judgment, and reason. Connect **observed failure -> diagnosis -> corrected canonical section -> regression -> new result**. If the baseline was not executed, the new sample proves only its own result; do not announce comparative improvement. A documentary test without a consumer must not be renamed an interface test.

For completed-design claims, inspect the actual output against the brief: composition, content, behavior, and affected sizes/states. Compare baseline/candidate renders under equivalent conditions before claiming better design. To claim consistency, repeat independent trials from the same initial conditions, preserve every output, and report outcomes and sample limits. A good plan, correct specialist choice, or passing package checks does not establish design quality or consistency. Keep a plan-only case separate from a fixture requiring an implemented and observed destination.

When available, record duration, retries, relevant calls, and host-reported usage. Unavailable model/configuration details remain unknown; estimated and billed costs are distinct and require units/sources. File bytes are not consumed tokens. Do not turn three different tasks into three repetitions of the same case or an attempt adjusted after an error into an independent trial. Small samples support bounded reporting, not a percentage promise.
