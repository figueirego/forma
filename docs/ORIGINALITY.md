# Forma system and library

Forma connects a coordinator, eleven design specialists, reusable components, reference collections and validation tools. Its instructions and tools are maintained in `source/forma/` and distributed through generated plugin and Agent Skills packages.

## Skills and coordination

The coordinator selects the relevant skill, carries the destination's approved decisions through the work and verifies the resulting implementation. Foundations, Interface, Product, Objects 3D, Graphs & Data, Motion, Platforms, Brand, Reference, Validation and Handoff have distinct responsibilities.

The [coordination contract](../source/forma/references/routing.md) defines their boundaries. The [capability map](../source/forma/references/sources.md) connects each responsibility to a resource and its evidence. The [use-case guide](../source/forma/references/source-use-cases.md) provides concrete applications.

## Components and refinement

The clean shell, approved book integration, articulated passport and appearance editor provide reusable mechanisms with explicit adapters and dependencies. The [component contracts](../source/forma/references/reusable-components.md) and [book integration](../source/forma/references/book-integration.md) explain what travels with each mechanism.

The refinement method also applies to other objects and interfaces: inspect the relevant layers, establish comparison conditions, identify the mismatch, correct its cause and verify the result in the destination. Shared methods do not mean that every object uses the same geometry or renderer.

## Patterns, sites and portfolios

Forma patterns describe its own resources, use cases, adaptation limits and checks. Website and portfolio indexes organize external destinations for investigation; they do not claim authorship of those sites. Observed experiences retain the evidence and limitations of specific recorded findings.

Use `references.mjs --list` for patterns, `--sites --list` for website candidates, `--portfolios --list` for portfolio candidates and `--observed` for recorded observations. See the [reference library](../source/forma/references/reference-library.md) for selection and research procedures and [library evolution](REFERENCE-HISTORY.md) for capability milestones.

## Contribution and verification

Keep the relevant Forma owner, implementation resource, adaptation decision and check connected. Follow [authorship and incorporation](../source/forma/references/authorship.md) when adding a reusable finding. Preserve source identities and applicable asset licenses.

Package checks verify structure, links, resources and distribution consistency. Rendered acceptance requires observing the consumer. Report local implementation, executed checks and publication separately; a catalog entry or passing structural test is not proof of universal design quality.
