# Split product-story landing

Use when an offer needs a clear message beside a concrete product explanation. Discover by `split product landing` or `landing produto`; apply the [destination identity contract](capability-reuse.md). Avoid this composition when the product cannot be represented honestly or a simpler reading flow serves the audience better.

## Mechanism

1. Give the message and primary action their own column. Place a product story beside it with one dominant current-step card and two quieter supporting cards, such as input and outcome.
2. Keep the product field visually quiet. Use destination surfaces and accent roles; a yellow field, black panel, telephony UI or source headline is not part of the mechanism.
3. Preserve a clear foreground/background hierarchy without obscuring essential information. On narrow screens, stack message, action and the story in reading order rather than scaling desktop cards into illegibility.
4. Extend the narrative through task-oriented feature tabs, short steps, answers and one clear conversion boundary. Tabs need semantic selection, keyboard support and meaningful content, not decorative alternate headings.
5. Product interactions are either connected to the real domain or explicitly illustrative. A local form that only validates is not a submitted lead. Do not add made-up statistics, testimonials, payment success or outbound calls to make the page persuasive.

## Destination bindings and proof

Reuse approved fonts, copy, logo, colors, icon family and action style. Keep identity variables separate from content/state. Pick proportions, spacing and card scale for the actual text and breakpoints; the source's measurements are reference values. Respect reduced motion and leave the CTA usable without decorative movement. Never append personal form data to an external navigation URL.

The [recorded source](helloura-experience.md#split-landing-and-conversion) and [profile](../assets/reference-profiles/helloura-experience.json) preserve one approved implementation. Its complete source is a private optional fidelity kit. This public contract is an independent recipe, not a bundled product page. The [Soft Glass landing](soft-glass.md) is a different available specimen and must not be described as the same composition.

Verify reading order, card overlap, long copy, small screens, keyboard tabs, reduced motion, real CTA destinations and the form's actual submission boundary. Check the destination identity in both supported modes; old source approval is not new acceptance.
